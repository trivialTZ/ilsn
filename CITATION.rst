Citation and acknowledgements
=============================

This source distribution accompanies an ongoing ilSN simulation study by
Xianzhe “TZ” Tang and collaborators. A publication-specific citation will
be added when the manuscript has a public reference. Do not cite a placeholder
DOI or treat the example results as a published Roman forecast.

In scientific applications, cite the paper describing the chosen SN
simulation/standardization, lens models, selection and population assumptions,
as well as NumPy, SciPy and Astropy. The response dataset in ``data/`` describes
its simulation scope and limitations in ``data/README.rst``.

PSFr and SLSim provided examples of a clear scientific software interface.
Their code was studied for organization; this package's physical equations
and likelihoods were extracted and reorganized from the ilSN research code.
